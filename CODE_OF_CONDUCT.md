# Code of conduct

BeeBox is a small project for people having a bad day with their computer.
Please bring the same patience to each other.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many people opening issues just lost work.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public channels unless privacy is needed.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people, who don't follow
this. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact on
the repository's profile; reports are kept confidential.
